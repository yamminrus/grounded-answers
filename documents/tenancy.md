# Tenancy agreement, 14 Rosewell Street

The tenancy began on 1 September 2025 and runs for 24 months.
Rent is 1,450 pounds per calendar month, due on the first of the month.
The deposit of 1,740 pounds is held with the Deposit Protection Service.
The landlord is responsible for the boiler, the roof and the external windows.
The tenant may keep one cat with written permission, which was given on 3 October 2025.
Two months notice is required to end the tenancy early.
