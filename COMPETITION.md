|---|---|
| public repository | |

## The two that are always missed

**The licence.** Most competitions require one and most submissions add it the
night before. It is a legal decision, not a formality.

**The before state.** If the project existed before the window, the rules
usually ask what changed during it. Captured on day one that is evidence.
Reconstructed at the end it is a story, and judges have read a hundred stories.

## The gate

`bash tools/gate_cycle.sh` must be green before anything is pushed public. If
it is red, the repository is not safe to publish, whatever the deadline says.
